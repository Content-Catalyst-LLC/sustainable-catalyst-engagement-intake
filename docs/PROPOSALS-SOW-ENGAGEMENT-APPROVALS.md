# Proposals, Statements of Work, and Engagement Approvals

## Purpose

This module governs the transition from a qualified inquiry and completed review process into a documented engagement. It provides operational evidence and does not replace legal advice, external contracts, electronic signatures, invoicing, or payment systems.

## Proposal governance

Proposal versions are immutable historical records. Only the current approved version can receive a sender action. Sender actions include receipt confirmation, change requests, acceptance, and decline. Each action records the actor, timestamp, version, attestation, and integrity hash.

## Statements of Work

A Statement of Work retains purpose, scope, deliverables, milestones, responsibilities, dependencies, acceptance criteria, change control, communication expectations, data handling, intellectual-property terms, open-source boundaries, fees, dates, termination terms, and approved attachments. Revisions create new versions. A sender-approved SOW is locked.

## Engagement conversion

Conversion requires: a proposal in the contracted state, external contract evidence, a sender-approved SOW, an authorized administrator, and typed confirmation. Conversion is idempotent and preserves the original proposal and SOW.

## Change requests

Change requests retain requested change, reason, scope impact, timeline impact, fee impact, decision, and related proposal, SOW, or engagement. Governed transitions preserve history.

## Sender Portal boundary

Senders receive only approved proposal and SOW content and deliberate decision controls. Internal pricing analysis, approval history, private notes, risk assessments, integrity hashes, assignments, and drafts remain private.
