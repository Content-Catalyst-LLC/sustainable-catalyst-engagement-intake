# Pilot and Public Launch Operations — v1.0.3

## Controlled pilot requirement

Complete at least five controlled inquiries across the supported public paths. The evidence checklist must include:

- general inquiry
- Advisory inquiry
- Sustainable AI Assurance inquiry
- private document upload and authorized retrieval
- administrative notification received
- sender acknowledgment received
- Sender Portal invitation, access, isolation, expiry, and logout behavior

## Routed entry URLs

Use the Contact page as the canonical submission endpoint and append `?engagement=<route>`. Supported route keys are documented in `SHORTCODES.md` and displayed in Platform Overview.

## Email evidence

Live Validation proves only that WordPress accepted the message. Confirm external receipt in a monitored inbox and record a non-secret reference such as a ticket number, message date, or mailbox note. Do not paste credentials or full sensitive message bodies.

## Operational review

Before Production, clear or explicitly resolve:

- failed communication attempts
- quarantine items
- active Sender Portal lockouts
- overdue operational work
- unresolved critical reliability events

## Production decision

Production remains a typed, capability-protected human action. A 100% score is necessary but is not sufficient unless the live validation, inbox, pilot, operational, and backup evidence are all current.
