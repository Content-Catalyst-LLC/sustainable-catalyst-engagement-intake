# Product Roadmap

## Stable release

### v1.0.0 — Unified Contact and Engagement Platform

Complete governed contact-to-engagement platform with stable compatibility, unified administration, launch readiness, migration provenance, public entry routing, secure sender collaboration, review, fit, Teams scheduling, proposals, engagement handoff, analytics, reliability, privacy, and Workflow Core integration.

## Post-1.0 maintenance direction

- v1.0.x: production activation, migration, browser, accessibility, and hosting compatibility patches
- v1.1.0: controlled cross-plugin adapter implementations and handoff acknowledgments
- v1.2.0: organization and multi-contact engagement records without sender ranking
- v1.3.0: governed service delivery workspace integration
- v2.0.0: mature multi-service engagement operations after independent security and accessibility review

All future work retains human-controlled acceptance, fit, proposal, contract, and engagement activation boundaries.
