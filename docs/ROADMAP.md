# Product Roadmap

## Current release

### v1.1.0 — Advisory Operations and Engagement Lifecycle

Governed lifecycle stages, qualification, internal workspaces, Teams/proposal/engagement linkage, sender-safe status publishing, tasks, communication templates, privacy integration, operational metrics, and extended production gating.

## Next maintenance release

### v1.1.1 — Lifecycle Reliability and Notification Patch

- live-host migration and backfill findings
- transition concurrency and recovery refinements
- reminder delivery diagnostics and resend controls
- portal lifecycle rendering and cache compatibility
- task, due-date, timezone, browser, mobile, and accessibility fixes
- proposal/meeting/engagement linkage repair tools

## Planned feature releases

### v1.2.0 — Proposals, Statements of Work, and Engagement Approvals

Governed scope, deliverables, assumptions, pricing, expiration, approval, and external-contract evidence without automatic acceptance, signatures, invoicing, or payment collection.

### v1.3.0 — Microsoft Teams and Calendar Coordination

Human-approved scheduling windows, Teams meeting creation, calendar references, agendas, pre-meeting requests, post-meeting notes, and follow-up coordination.

### v1.4.0 — Client Workspace and Secure Collaboration

Deliberately published engagement milestones, secure document exchange, requested-information workflows, proposal notices, meeting context, and sender follow-up.

### v1.5.0 — Engagement Analytics and Service Intelligence

Aggregate demand, service routing, response, qualification, proposal, acceptance, workload, and delivery metrics with cohort suppression and no sender ranking.

### v2.0.0 — Integrated Advisory and Institutional Engagement Platform

Mature multi-service engagement operations after independent security, privacy, accessibility, and operational review.

All future releases retain human-controlled qualification, acceptance, proposal publication, contract attestation, scheduling, engagement activation, and production promotion.
