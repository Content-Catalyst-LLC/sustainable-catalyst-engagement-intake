# Product Roadmap

## Current release

### v1.7.0 — Billing, Invoicing, and Payment Handoffs

Engagement-linked billing profiles, versioned invoices, human-reviewed issue and void transitions, external HTTPS payment handoffs, sender-safe invoice views, and privacy-safe operational billing metrics without storing payment instruments.

## Previous release

### v1.6.0 — Engagement Analytics and Service Intelligence

Privacy-safe aggregate demand, routing, cycle-time, support-friction, proposal, engagement, workspace, milestone, deliverable, and completion metrics with minimum-cohort suppression, hashed snapshots, and human-reviewed service-intelligence findings.

## Planned engagement releases

### v1.7.1 — Billing, Invoice Versioning, and Payment-Handoff Reliability Patch

Live-host migration findings, invoice-version concurrency, provider-event replay, reconciliation consistency, sender projection, privacy export, and operational dashboard reliability.

### v1.8.0 — Engagement Financial Operations and Accounting Integration

Controlled exports and typed handoffs to external accounting systems, revenue recognition status, credit and refund records, and reconciliation evidence without storing payment credentials or replacing the accounting ledger.

### v2.0.0 — Integrated Advisory, Support, and Institutional Engagement Platform

Mature multi-service engagement operations after independent security, privacy, accessibility, and operational review.

## Coordinated product-support roadmap

Feature Suggestions remains the public Product Support and Feedback Platform, including the Support Knowledge Base, known issues, guided resolution, public suggestions, voting, and product intelligence. Contact and Engagement remains the private inquiry, support-case, communication, file, Sender Portal, proposal, scheduling, and client-workspace layer.
